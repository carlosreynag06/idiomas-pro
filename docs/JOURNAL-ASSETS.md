## Dedicated journal artwork — readability revision

These six separate article covers were created with the built-in ImageGen tool. None reuse the homepage hero. Each final image is saved as an original PNG in `assets/imagegen/` and an optimized WebP in `public/images/`.

- Original: `assets/imagegen/journal-private-lessons-original.png`
- Website asset: `public/images/journal-private-lessons.webp`
- Mode: built-in ImageGen.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium language-learning journal article cover, landscape 3:2. Theme: what makes a private language lesson valuable. Overhead editorial photograph of a cream table during an adult one-to-one language lesson: two different pairs of hands actively working with one open notebook, blue pencil held by learner, tutor using a yellow card to explain a phrase. A deep cobalt workbook without visible text and several pale citron vocabulary cards arranged asymmetrically. Strong beautiful diagonal daylight and shadows, tactile paper, rich natural realistic skin tones. Adult hands with accurate anatomy. Sophisticated art direction, cobalt ivory citron palette, subtle film grain. No faces, no coffee, no travel props, no plants, no flags, no screens, no tourism, no watermark or legible words. Frame closely on the learning interaction. Distinct and visually interesting, not generic stationery stock.
